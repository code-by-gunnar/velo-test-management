   - Technical data: IP address, browser type (server logs, not tracked)

4. **Who We Share With** — Sub-processors only:

   - Vercel (frontend hosting)

   - Resend (transactional email)

   - Account data: retained while account is active

8. **Changes to This Policy** — We will update this page. Last updated date shown.

9. **Contact** — privacy@runvelo.app
